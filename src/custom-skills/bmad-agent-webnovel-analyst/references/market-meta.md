# Market Meta

The outcome is packaging and platform intelligence for the genre, written so an author or pipeline could position and package a title from this report alone: where the genre's readers actually are, the tag combinations its top titles carry, title and blurb conventions with observed examples, length and update-cadence expectations, and monetization norms (Patreon chapter-lead patterns, Kindle Unlimited stubbing, platform contracts).

Algorithm claims — what Rising Stars rewards, how trending weights follows versus views — are rarely verifiable from outside. Report them as hypotheses with their source and recency, never as observed fact. The line between "observed on top titles" and "believed by the author community" must stay visible in every section.

Write the report to `{agent.report_output_path}/<genre>-market-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, methodology line included.
